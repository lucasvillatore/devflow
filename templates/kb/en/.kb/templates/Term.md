# Template: Term

## What it is

The meaning of a business term. It contains no rules.

## Where it lives

- Used in one context only: `Business contexts/<Context>/Glossary/<Term>.md`.
- Used in more than one context: moves up to `Glossary/<Term>.md`, at the root.
- Same word with different meanings: each context has its own file.
- Term without a file yet: listed under "To define" in the glossary's `index.md`.

## Sections

- `# Definition`: one sentence that says what the term is.
- When there are variations: one `##` per group, with one sentence explaining the group, and each variation in a Name/Description block. See `style.md`.
- `# Example`: one sentence that explains how to read the diagrams and one `##` per scenario.

## Does not contain

- Rules, tables, "Avoid".
- Links to who uses the term (see the backlinks). A variation may point to the document it names.
